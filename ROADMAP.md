The companion is a 0.2.0 preview. See [STATUS.md](docs/STATUS.md) for implemented paths and [ACCEPTANCE.md](docs/ACCEPTANCE.md) for evidence and remaining native checks.

1. Complete Windows/ingame acceptance of chat, takeover, bridge, screenshot, vnavmesh, Lifestream and menu callbacks. Use exact tested builds and representative zones; record failures.
2. Finish the first house-visit vertical: saved multi-step route, explicit semantic door-entry recipe, scene analysis and a sourced shared episode. Add visual entity projection and supported territory/instance handling.
3. Add reviewed migration preview for legacy people, persona and memories; expand sessions, groups, relationship management and correction/version history.
4. Polish ingame draft/configuration editing, onboarding, web route/task editor and deterministic full conversation replay.
5. Choose and implement the optional native live-audio provider, devices and interruption without changing public/private output channels.
6. Expand individual certified abilities for questing, purchases, retainers and deterministic combat controllers. Their permissions and completion checks remain separate.

A 1.0 label requires the defined complete companion scenario and the real-client matrix. Build success alone does not trigger the label.
